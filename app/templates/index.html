Customer Churn Prediction

For Food Delivery & Quick Commerce Platforms

About

This application uses machine learning to predict customer churn for food delivery and quick commerce platforms like Swiggy, Zomato, Zepto, and Blinkit. By analyzing customer behavior patterns, we can identify customers who are at risk of churning, allowing businesses to take proactive measures to retain them.

Our model is trained on customer data including order frequency, average order value, delivery issues, and many other factors that influence customer retention.

How It Works

Our churn prediction system works in three simple steps:

  1. Input customer data through our prediction form
  2. Our machine learning model analyzes the data
  3. Receive churn probability and risk assessment

The model identifies patterns that indicate a customer might stop using the service, allowing businesses to take targeted retention actions.
